About This Book

Mindset Mastery: 100 Shifts for Lasting Success is a practical guide to transforming how you think, work, and live. Across 53 chapters organized into 5 parts, you'll learn to build discipline over motivation, embrace failure as feedback, develop emotional intelligence, harness the power of consistency, and create lasting success — one shift at a time.
